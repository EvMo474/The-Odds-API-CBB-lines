Second-half cover against each book's line about 5 minutes into the break, at -110. The bucket is the cold team's pregame or closing role. The FG% gap is the cold team's first-half FG% shortfall. Games with a suspect home/away mix-up are left out.

Halftime games

The cold team is the one that shot worse in the first half. Under each game, ↳ lists every book's second-half line for the cold team, high to low: the live full-game spread about 5 minutes into the break, plus the cold team's halftime margin. Positive means the cold team is the second-half underdog. Best 2H is the top of that list. Live line is the cold team's median live full-game spread. ½ Close is half the closing spread for the cold team, which is what the second half would be worth if the market only split the closing line in two. Mkt shift is the median 2H line minus ½ Close: negative means the market is giving the cold team fewer second-half points than half the closing line, positive means more. Closing line is DraftKings at tip when captured, otherwise the earlier pregame line. Scores are away–home. The 2H margin is from the cold team's side.
